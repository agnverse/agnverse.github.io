August 2026

Suude Bayram Successfully Defends Her M.Sc. Thesis

Our research group graduate student Suude Bayram successfully defended her Master's thesis entitled “Identification of Radio-Loud Quasars Using LOFAR LoTSS Data” on July 28, 2026.

The thesis work was evaluated and approved by defense jury members Prof. Dr. Ersin Göğüş, Asst. Prof. Dr. M. Kürşad Yıldız, and thesis advisor Asst. Prof. Dr. Nurten Filiz Ak.

We warmly congratulate Suude on her productive and successful thesis defense, and wish her continued success in her academic career.
