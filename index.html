AGNverse

Erciyes Üniversitesi · Aktif Galaksi · Araştırma Grubu

Erciyes Üniversitesi AGN Araştırma Grubu olarak aktif galaksi çekirdeklerini, x-ışın, morötesi, optik, kızılötesi ve radyo dalgaboylarında elde edilen gözlemlerle inceliyoruz. Bu gözlemlerden elde edilen verileri analiz ederek, AGN yaspısını, evrimini ve çevreleriyle etkileşimlerini anlamaya çalışıyoruz. Araştırmalarımızda istatistiksel yöntemler, makine öğrenmesi ve bilimsel yazılım geliştirme tekniklerini kullanıyoruz. Araştırmalarımızın temel amacı, süper kütleli kara deliklerin büyüme süreçlerini, galaksi evrimini ve evrendeki enerjik astrofiziksel sistemleri anlamaktır. Araştırmalar Veri kaynakaları arasında XMM-Newton, Chandra, NuSTAR, XRISM, SDSS, LAMOST, LoTSS, RACS, MeerKAT verileriyle aktif galaktik çekirdeklerin zamanlama ve spektral yapısını çözümlüyoruz.

Research Vision

My research aims to understand how supermassive black holes grow, interact with their environments, and influence galaxy evolution. By combining multi-wavelength observations, statistical methods, and scientific software, I investigate the physical processes governing Active Galactic Nuclei and other energetic astrophysical systems. I believe that modern astronomy advances through the integration of observations, computation, and open science.

Research Areas

My research combines observational astronomy, computational methods, and data science to investigate the physical nature of compact objects and galaxy evolution.

Active Galactic Nuclei

Structure, evolution, variability, radio emission, giant radio quasars, and candidate binary supermassive black holes.

Multi-wavelength Astronomy

Optical, ultraviolet, X-ray, and radio observations for understanding energetic astrophysical systems.

Astrostatistics

Statistical inference, uncertainty estimation, Bayesian methods, and large astronomical datasets.

Machine Learning

Classification, anomaly detection, feature extraction, and intelligent analysis of astronomical observations.

Scientific Programming

Python, MATLAB, R, reproducible workflows, open-source software, and scientific computing.

Binary Stellar Systems

Orbital modelling, spectroscopy, and observational analysis of binary and multiple stellar systems.

Scientific Software

I develop scientific software to support astronomical research, automate data analysis, and promote reproducible science. My projects range from spectroscopy pipelines and orbital modelling tools to machine learning applications and interactive educational resources for astronomy.
